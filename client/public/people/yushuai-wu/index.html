About关于我

I am a Researcher at πAI Lab and the Shanghai Academy of Artificial Intelligence for Science. I work on biomedical AI that connects cells, tissues and patients: reconstructing spatial single-cell data as virtual cells, predicting drug response with virtual patients, and building models that read pathology, imaging and omics together.我现任 πAI Lab 研究员，同时就职于上海科学智能研究院。研究聚焦连接细胞、组织与患者的生物医学人工智能：把空间单细胞数据重建为虚拟细胞，以虚拟患者预测药物响应，并构建融合病理、影像与组学的多模态模型

I received my bachelor’s degree and Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the School of Medicine, Tsinghua University, where I built high-throughput multi-omics platforms for organoid drug screening. I then worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, and co-founded PharMolix.我在清华大学医学院获得生物医学工程学士与博士学位，博士期间搭建面向类器官药物筛选的高通量多组学平台；此后在清华大学智能产业研究院从事博士后研究，并参与创立水木分子

Research directions研究方向

Spatial Virtual Cells空间虚拟细胞

Reconstructing spatial single-cell transcriptomes across spatial technologies to recover tissue programs hidden in raw data, from prognostic immune niches to fibroblast heterogeneity at the tumor leading edge跨空间技术重建单细胞分辨率的空间转录组，还原原始数据中被掩盖的组织程序，从预后相关的免疫生态位到肿瘤侵袭前沿的成纤维细胞异质性

Virtual Patients and Drug Response虚拟患者与药物响应

Carrying drug-response evidence from cells to patients, from high-throughput organoid screening to models that evaluate drugs and indications at population level让药物响应证据从细胞走向患者，从高通量类器官筛选到在人群层面评估药物与适应证的模型

Multimodal Biomedical Models多模态生物医学模型

Integrating pathology, radiology, omics and language into foundation and fusion models for subtyping, prognosis and target discovery把病理、影像、组学与语言整合进基础模型与融合模型，服务于分子分型、预后判断与靶点发现
