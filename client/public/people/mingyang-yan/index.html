关于我About

我目前在广东智慧医学国际研究院担任 AI 产品与全栈工程师，于 2026 年 7 月加入，主要负责 OmniMind 和 AI4S News 的开发。I joined the International Academy of Phronesis Medicine (Guangdong) in July 2026 as an AI Product & Full-Stack Engineer, primarily developing OmniMind and AI4S News.

2021—2025 年，我就读于北京理工大学网络空间安全专业。毕业后加入中移互联网，担任中间号产品经理，随后转入研究院。I studied Cyberspace Security at Beijing Institute of Technology from 2021 to 2025. After graduation, I joined China Mobile Internet as a product manager for virtual number services, before moving to the institute.

我参与了 OmniFigure 的研究，探索数据可视化的统一表征与不同可视化形式之间的共通表达。该工作于 2026 年 9 月投稿至 ICLR 2027。I co-authored OmniFigure, exploring a unified representation for data visualization across different visual forms. The work was submitted to ICLR 2027 in September 2026.

此外，我参与了开源项目 Idea2Paper。I also participated in the open-source project Idea2Paper.
