About关于我

I am an AI Agent Systems Architect at the International Academy of Phronesis Medicine (Guangdong), where I work on person-centric agents and agent reasoning for autonomous scientific discovery.我现任广东智慧医学国际研究院 AI 智能体系统架构师，研究以人为中心的智能体，以及面向自主科学发现的智能体推理。

I co-founded QuantaAlpha, a nonprofit research institute whose team published more than 20 papers at NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL and other leading AI conferences in one year. Earlier, as an AI Scientist at a Private Family Office, I worked on 7B–70B language-model training, agent systems, retrieval-augmented generation, safety, and evaluation.我曾联合创立非营利研究机构 QuantaAlpha，带领团队在一年内于 NeurIPS、ICLR、ACL 等人工智能顶级会议发表论文 20 余篇。此前，我在私人家族办公室担任AI 科学家，参与 7B–70B 大模型训练，以及智能体、检索增强生成、安全与评测系统的研发。

Research directions研究方向

Agent Reasoning and Recursive Self-Improvement for Scientific Discovery面向科学发现的智能体推理与递归自我改进

Exploring how agent reasoning and recursive self-improvement (RSI) can help systems propose and test hypotheses, discover new knowledge with human scientists, and move beyond optimizing a convenient benchmark score.研究智能体推理与递归自我改进（RSI）如何帮助系统提出并验证假设、与人类科学家协作发现新知识，并超越对单一基准分数的优化。

Person-Centric Agents and Digital Companions以人为中心的智能体与数字伴侣

Building agents that form a durable model of a person, remember what matters across time, respond with context, and take initiative when support is useful—toward digital companions that understand people rather than merely answer prompts.构建能够持续理解和记忆一个人、结合上下文做出恰当回应，并在真正有帮助时主动行动的智能体，推动数字伴侣从“回答问题”走向“理解人”。
